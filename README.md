# Blue Collar King — demo brand-home site

Demo-first pitch site for **Blue Collar King**, the commercial HVAC business-coaching brand of **Matt Murray**. Built from Matt's own public funnel content (go.bluecollarkings.net) so Wilson can DM it to him as a proposed brand home behind his ads.

**Live:** https://wilsonramstead.github.io/blue-collar-king/

## What this is
This is *not* a local service business — it's a personal-brand coaching company. The page is the "brand home" a skeptical $2M+ HVAC owner lands on when they Google Matt to vet him before buying. Goal: authority, proof, one clear path to the funnel (the free masterclass).

Because it's a personal brand, featuring Matt Murray is intentional and correct here (the usual no-owner-section rule does not apply to coaching brands).

## Design
- **Fonts:** Cinzel (display — regal, fits "King") + Inter (body).
- **Palette:** deep navy (#0b1f3a / #071628), regal gold (#c9a24b / #e0be6e), off-white (#f6f1e6). Steel accents.
- **Motif:** subtle crown mark in header, dividers, and CTA band. Premium authority feel — not local-tradesman styling.
- Sticky navy header with a gold "Free Masterclass" CTA. No fixed bottom bar. No contact forms.

## Sections
Hero → authority stat bar (since 2003 · $4–5M/yr · A-player team · self-running) → Matt Murray founder section (with his verbatim "not some B.S. coach or guru" quote) → six offering pillars → free Zoom masterclass ("Commercial Client Sales Engine", 5-stage system) → pull-quote divider → four real testimonials (Chadwell Comfort, Engle Services, Liebi Mechanical, Able Heating and Air) → who it's for ($2M → $10M–$30M) → final CTA band → footer.

## CTAs
- **Primary:** Register for the free masterclass → https://go.bluecollarkings.net
- **Secondary:** DM on Instagram → https://instagram.com/therealbluecollarking
- No phone or email is published (none known).

## Content rules honored
- Every fact is from Matt's own funnel — no invented credentials, revenue guarantees, client counts, or income promises.
- Results language is framed as Matt's own claims ("the exact system Matt uses in his own company"), never as guarantees. Footer carries an explicit no-guarantee disclaimer.
- `V1 DRAFT` comment near the top: headshot/photos, contact details, and program names to be confirmed with Matt.

## Demo / SEO notes
